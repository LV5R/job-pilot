Job Description - Early Careers Graduate Programme 2027  
 	  
Department: FIL Platforms 
Location:  	Bengaluru / Gurugram 
 
Who we are: 
 
Do you feel passionate about becoming an investor and making a real difference to our clients’ futures?  
We’ve been helping clients plan and invest for their futures for over 50 years. From more than 25 locations around the world, we provide investment services and retirement expertise to everyone from central banks to private individuals.  
We’re known for acting with integrity and thinking for the long-term. And across our organisation, we foster a welcoming, caring and inclusive culture where people feel respected, trusted and able to be themselves.  
 
The Team you will join: 
 
FIL Platforms is the engine that powers Fidelity's global business, bringing together technology, digital, cybersecurity, data and platform capabilities to deliver innovative, scalable and secure solutions for our customers and associates worldwide. Operating across multiple global locations, our teams work together to build and support the platforms, products and services that enable Fidelity's investment, retirement and workplace businesses.  
 
By combining engineering excellence, modern technology practices, AI-enabled innovation and a customer-first mindset, FIL Platforms plays a critical role in driving Fidelity's digital transformation, enhancing client experiences and supporting long-term business growth. As a graduate, you'll join a collaborative global community with opportunities to contribute across software engineering, data, cloud, cybersecurity and technology transformation initiatives while developing the skills needed to shape the future of financial technology. 
 
What you’ll be doing: 
 
Working as part of agile technology teams, you'll contribute to the design, development, testing and deployment of innovative solutions across software engineering, data, cloud, cybersecurity and various technology domains. You'll apply modern engineering practices to solve real business challenges, leverage AI-powered tools to enhance productivity and contribute to the delivery of secure, scalable and customer-focused technology solutions. As your experience grows, you'll collaborate with stakeholders, influence technical decisions and help shape the future of technology at Fidelity. 
 
The difference you’ll make: 
 
As your skills, confidence, and functional expertise grow, you’ll become increasingly involved in key decisions that help our customers achieve their investment and financial planning goals. Through hands-on experience, you’ll build deep knowledge of the industry, products, and customer needs, enabling you to make a meaningful impact. You’ll also provide invaluable support to your team, contributing insights and expertise while continuing to develop your professional capabilities. 
 
How you’ll be supported: 
 
Our Graduate Programme is designed to provide a strong foundation for a successful career in technology through a combination of structured learning, hands-on experience and continuous support. From day one, you'll participate in a 
comprehensive induction and technology bootcamp that helps you build core capabilities across software engineering, data, cloud, cybersecurity and modern delivery practices, while also developing the professional skills needed to thrive in a collaborative environment. A key focus of the programme is building AI Fluency, equipping graduates with the ability to responsibly leverage AI-powered tools to enhance productivity, accelerate software development, improve analysis and solve real business challenges. As you progress, you'll apply these skills in agile delivery teams, working on live projects that create tangible business value while gaining exposure to a variety of technology disciplines.  
 
Beyond technical development, you'll benefit from ongoing training, leadership interactions, networking opportunities and workshops focused on communication, adaptability and personal effectiveness. Support is embedded throughout the experience, with dedicated managers, mentors and buddies helping you navigate your career journey, while a vibrant early careers community provides opportunities to build lasting professional networks. By the end of the programme, you'll have developed both the technical expertise and AI-enabled ways of working needed to succeed in an increasingly technology-driven world, with access to continued learning and career opportunities across Fidelity. 

We seek graduates who are curious, empathetic, enthusiastic about learning, and willing to dive into new challenges. 
Creativity and an eye for detail are also crucial. Additionally, as we operate globally, proficiency in English is essential.

AI Fluency: A Core Capability for Every Technology Graduate. At Fidelity, we believe AI Fluency will be as fundamental to future technology careers as programming and problem-solving skills. We are seeking graduates who can confidently and responsibly apply AI tools to enhance their effectiveness, improve outcomes and accelerate innovation. As an AIfluent graduate, you may: 
•	Use AI-powered tools to accelerate software development and testing. 
•	Leverage AI to analyse data, identify patterns and generate insights. 
•	Improve productivity through automation and intelligent workflows. 
•	Create effective prompts and critically evaluate AI-generated outputs. 
•	Apply AI responsibly while considering security, privacy, risk and governance requirements. 
•	Collaborate with teams to identify practical opportunities for AI-enabled solutions. 
•	Continuously adapt to emerging technologies and evolving ways of working.
